# Photorealism direction

User-supplied reference: `cockpit-reference.png` (a still image, not a supplied video).

Desired visual qualities visible in the reference:
- Convincing cockpit scale, depth, dense instrumentation, and varied surfaces.
- Bright sunlit sky balanced against a much darker cockpit interior.
- Layered clouds, atmospheric depth, distant terrain, and a readable horizon.
- Canopy reflections and subtle surface imperfections that suggest real glass.
- Believable aircraft silhouette, shading, and spatial relationship to the cockpit.

The image uses strong cyan/blue colour and dramatic contrast. Treat these as
art direction, not proof of physically accurate exposure or colour. Seek coherent
realism rather than copying saturation or adding excessive scratches and glare.
Motion, temporal stability, and performance cannot be assessed from this still.

This reference does not request implementation of every depicted element.
