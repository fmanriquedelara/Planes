## Prioritized critique and acceptance criteria

All appearance implications below are source-derived hypotheses until captured.

### P1 — Lighting and atmosphere lack deliberate art direction

Evidence: Flight.tscn has an unconfigured procedural sky and minimal Environment.
There is no configured aerial perspective. This risks a flat synthetic sky and
weak separation between near aircraft and distant scenery, unlike the layered
depth and bright-sky/dark-cockpit relationship in the reference.

Recommendation: one restrained daylight pass with coordinated sky/sun colours,
filmic highlight rolloff, balanced ambient contribution, light atmospheric haze,
and conservative contact shading. Do not turn the image's cyan saturation into
a physical target or conceal scenery with heavy fog. Volumetric clouds are a
separate task, not implied by enabling fog.

Acceptance: identical cockpit and external views before/after, with scene, camera,
resolution and settings recorded; cockpit instruments remain readable, sky and
aircraft highlights retain detail, shadows anchor nearby surfaces, distant scenery
fades gradually rather than being obscured. Confirm in motion before final pass.

### P1 — Wing material replacement loses imported material information

Evidence: Plane.gd replaces the airframe material with an inline shader. It
samples albedo, normal and metallic texture unconditionally; does not preserve
albedo colour, roughness/metallic factors, texture channel selections or AO; and
assumes metallic and roughness share a packed texture. Normal depth and specular
are hardcoded. The original material values are printed but not retained.

Risk: incorrect paint/metal appearance and inconsistent response to new lighting.
The exact visible effect depends on the imported material and remains unverified.

Recommendation: a separate bounded material-preservation pass that keeps wing
deformation and dynamics intact, with proper factors, optional-texture fallbacks
and actual imported channel semantics. Inspect imported materials first.

Acceptance: at zero deformation the shader matches the imported material in
controlled lighting; absent maps have valid fallbacks; deformation still works;
no changes to flight equations, controller mappings or camera movement.

### P2 — Edge stability has no explicit rendering policy

Evidence: project.godot has no rendering section or explicit AA settings.
Fine cockpit edges, silhouettes and distant architecture are likely sensitive
to aliasing, but shimmer has not been observed.

Recommendation: start with moderate MSAA appropriate to Forward Plus. Assess
thin geometry in motion; do not enable many expensive effects without evidence.

Acceptance: compare the same resolution and camera motion; record rendering
cost only if actually measured. Ensure fine instruments remain sharp.

### P2 — World scale and cockpit detail need rendered inspection

Evidence: mixed city/Brackley scales, hidden ground/water and no explicit current
camera. These warrant inspecting placement, horizon continuity and cockpit
framing; they do not establish that assets are wrong.

Recommendation: defer terrain replacement, cockpit remodeling and canopy effects
until baseline captures show the specific gaps. Do not unhide placeholder water
or rescale imported scenes as a guess.

Acceptance: captures establish plausible building-to-aircraft scale, no abrupt
ground gaps, usable cockpit framing and no visible near-plane clipping.
