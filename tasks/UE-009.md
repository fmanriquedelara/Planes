# UE-009 — Godot flight model, gamepad and clear canopy bow

Milu owns the port, controller input and runtime verification. Vision separated
the Eurofighter bow mesh groups. Stark reviews the port and renders.

- Keep the C++ model independent of engine headers and make its state, inputs
  and step size explicit for a future FMU wrapper.
- Port the equations and effective lift clamp from Godot/Plane.gd. Use a 120 Hz
  Unreal adapter, with caller supplied gust samples and coordinate conversion.
- Map the gamepad from Godot/project.godot, including the held gun sound.
  Preserve keyboard and mouse bindings and the three Unreal camera views.
- Offer B and a pause menu button in the detailed Eurofighter. Apply the clear
  finish to all four bow groups in the pilot view and restore opaque materials
  and shadows in exterior views.
- Ignore generated renders, comparison binaries and rebuildable Unreal
  imports. Preserve source assets and tracked files.

The C++ model compiled without Unreal and passed equilibrium, throttle, axis and
wing checks. Wing state is computed for future callers. The Unreal mesh does
not consume it. The new Unreal controls use the Godot dead zones, with input
injection for validation rather than a physical controller.

Unreal 5.8.4 at 3440×1440 passed all 44 checks on the detailed Eurofighter
map and the 39 applicable checks on the default Gripen map. Both builds use
the Godot audio asset. The detail evidence is under artifacts/ue008/detail
and the default evidence is under artifacts/runtime-3440x1440.

Milu and Stark inspected the opaque and clear pilot views, chase and canopy
mount. The clear view shows a faint translucent outline with the HUD intact.
The bow returns to opaque in the two exterior views. The default map SHA256
remained 6225020529B584221E8CCF8CE82A3B2B1DC71AF955639E51D4F0E58D3708B144.
