## Stark's baseline image critique

Stark inspected `artifacts/eurofighter-cockpit.png` against the supplied cockpit
references. Technical cockpit framing passes; photorealistic image quality still
needs work. Visible priorities:

1. Terrain: oversized regular flat field patches and straight roads lack fine
   vegetation, buildings and convincing scale variation. Add multiscale detail.
2. Cockpit: blurred baked panels/screens, mottled surfaces and faceted canopy
   geometry limit close-up realism. A better cockpit asset/texture set is needed.
3. Atmosphere: soft cloud wisps lack layered volume; water is uniformly blue.
   Review the new density/coverage presets before choosing further improvements.
4. Lighting: muted teal/brown balance lacks the reference's crisp bright exterior
   and selective warm reflections. Assess exposure/material response separately.

These are actual-image findings, not a claim that the new controls alone resolve
them. Terrain/asset replacement is outside this feature pass.
